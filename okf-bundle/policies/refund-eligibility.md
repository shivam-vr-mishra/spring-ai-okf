# Refund Eligibility

A customer may request a refund within 30 days of delivery. Items must be
returned in their original packaging with all accessories included.

## Defect Return

A return is classified as a **defect return** when any of the following apply:

1. The item arrived physically damaged or broken.
2. The item was dead on arrival or failed within the first 30 days of use.
3. The item delivered does not match the item ordered.
4. The item is missing components that were listed as included.

A defect return must be reported within 30 days of delivery. Defect returns
are refunded in full and are exempt from any deduction.
